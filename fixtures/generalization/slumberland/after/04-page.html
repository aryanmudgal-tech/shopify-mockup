Service And Repairs

Handling Damage (At Time Of Delivery)

  • Any damage to your home or product must be reported to your local store within 5 days.
  • We reserve the right to inspect the damage. Service may be denied if damage was discovered to have occurred after delivery.
  • Slumberland will either repair/replace the item or component, at our discretion.

Manufacturer Defects (Product Warranty Claims)

  • Product warranties vary by manufacturer.
  • Slumberland will help facilitate service based on the terms and conditions of the manufacturer’s warranty.
  • Slumberland reserves the right to inspect all warranty claims and will decide to repair or replace defective merchandise at our discretion – no refunds.
  • Slumberland will provide service within our delivery areas.
  • For the first 90 days, Slumberland will provide service at no charge.
  • After 90 days of the product being in your home, inspection fees, delivery fees, and technician labor costs may apply.
  • For items outside of the manufacturer’s warranty, Slumberland will provide a technician referral in your area.All “As-Is” merchandise sales are final. Manufacturer warranties do not apply.
  • Please refer to any manufacturer’s documentation pertaining to their warranty, care instructions and terms of use.

Refunds

  • Cash and Check refunds will be in the form of a check and issued within 10 business days from when the payment has posted to all bank accounts.
  • Bank/Credit card payments will be refunded back to the same card as the original payment was made. Banks vary on processing time of refunds. A Slumberland Gift card can be substituted if the original card is invalid.
